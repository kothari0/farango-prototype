Required Fields

  • Full Name, Email, Password, Confirm Password
  • Date of Birth (drives 18+ eligibility check)
  • Terms & Conditions checkbox — separate from Privacy Policy checkbox

System Behaviour

  • Sign Up is disabled until both legal checkboxes are ticked.
  • Inline error if DOB indicates under 18: "You must be 18 or older to join Farango."
  • Inline error on duplicate email: "An account with this email already exists."
  • On success → proceeds to Email Verification (OTP), never straight to dashboard.
Source: Feature 1 — Registration Entry, Email Registration, Terms & Policy Acceptance, Age Eligibility Verification.

Email Verification (OTP)

Blocking step before dashboard access
✉

Verify your email

We've sent a 4-digit code to samuelb@gmail.com. Enter it below to verify your account.
Wrong email? Edit

Didn't get the code? Resend in 00:30

Key Elements

  • 4-digit OTP input, auto-advance per digit
  • Resend action (cooldown timer)
  • Edit-email affordance so a mistyped address can be corrected without restarting sign up

System Behaviour

  • Email verification is mandatory before the platform dashboard is reachable.
  • Incorrect code shows inline error under the OTP boxes; no field reset.
  • Expired code state: boxes clear, message "This code has expired — request a new one."
Source: Feature 1 — Email Verification (Verification Email / OTP). Edit-email affordance flagged as a recommended addition during wireframe review.
