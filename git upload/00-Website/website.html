About Farango

Built by golfers, for golfers

We're building the home for golf's creator economy — where coaches, caddies and pros can teach, connect and earn, and where players can find real expertise instead of generic content.

Company Overview

Golf's dedicated creator platform

Farango was founded to give golf instruction and community a home built specifically for the sport — not a generic video platform repurposed for golf. Since launch we've grown a roster of verified coaches, caddies and pros serving a fast-growing community of players.

Mission & Vision

Growing the game, one creator at a time

Our mission is to give every golf coach, caddy and pro the tools to build a sustainable business teaching the sport they love — and to give every player direct access to real expertise.

Creator success. Community safety. Platform integrity.
